Come Through helps family cut through the noise without starting a phone call.

Create a private room. Hold to talk. Correct what you said. Send a priority message that plays on the other iPhone — even when they’re mid-game or mid-scroll.

WHY IT EXISTS
When someone is deep in headphones or focus mode, a ringing call can feel aggressive — and a silent text can get ignored. Come Through is the middle path: a short, intentional cut-in you control.

HOW IT WORKS
1. One person creates a room and shares the code
2. The other joins with that code
3. Hold to talk — speech becomes editable text
4. Fix names, tone, or typos
5. Send — their phone plays your clear message with priority

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
• Private room codes — share only with people you trust
• Correct-before-send so the message lands clean
• Priority playback designed for “I need you for a second”
• Works between two iPhones without a phone call

BEST WITH
Parent ↔ kid, co-parents, coaches, anyone who needs a respectful interrupt that still gets through.

PRIVACY
Microphone access is used only when you hold to talk and send. See the in-app Privacy Policy for details.
